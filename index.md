## What this project is about

This project investigates how financial sector development relates to economic growth across 216 countries between 2004 and 2021, using World Bank World Development Indicators data. The analysis is structured around a core research question and five sub-questions that together build a complete picture — from mapping the global financial landscape to stress-testing the finance-growth relationship through the 2008 financial crisis and the 2020 COVID-19 shock.

The target audience is policymakers, development economists, and international financial institutions (IMF, World Bank).

## Dataset at a glance

- **Source:** World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI)
- **Panel:** 216 countries × 18 years (2004–2021) = 3,831 observations
- **Key variables:** GDP growth, domestic credit, broad money, bank branches, ATMs, FDI inflows, inflation
- **Metadata:** Region (7) and Income Group (4) per country
